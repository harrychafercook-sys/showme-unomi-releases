# Show Me You Know Me

Android TV downloads for a local game for 2–8 players. The TV runs the game; phones connect over the same Wi-Fi using the on-screen QR codes. No PC is needed.

## Install

1. Open **Downloader by AFTVnews** on your Android TV.
2. Enter **8412656**, download the APK and choose **Install**. If Android asks, allow Downloader to install this app. The direct download link above is also available.
3. Open **Show Me You Know Me**, keep it open, and connect phones to the same Wi-Fi.
4. Scan a seat's QR code, enter a name and optionally take a selfie. Two ready players are needed to start.

Requires Android TV 6.0 or later and support for ARMv7 applications. Phones use their web browser. The app installs separately from TV Card Games.

## Play

The centre player draws five words and flips for Most or Least. Everyone orders the words privately on their phone. The top three count: an exact word and position earns 2 points; the right word in another position earns 1 point. Players reveal third, second and first at their own pace.
